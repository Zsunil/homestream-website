Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

1. About HomeStream

HomeStream is an Android media-player application intended for private family use. It can use Google authentication and Google Drive to identify the signed-in user and access media that account is authorized to view.

2. Information the app may process

  • Google account information: account identity made available by Google Sign-In, such as the account email, when Google authentication is used.
  • Google Drive media information: file names, identifiers, metadata, and media content needed to display and play files the signed-in account is permitted to access.
  • Local app data: supported profile information, playback positions, watch history, and app settings stored on the device.
  • Technical diagnostics: diagnostic information may be processed when troubleshooting errors. Authentication tokens and passwords should not be included in logs.

3. How information is used

Information is used to sign the user in, check access to media, display the media catalogue, play selected media, restore playback progress, and maintain app settings.

4. Local storage and synchronization

Personal playback information supported by the app is stored locally on the device. It is not automatically synchronized across devices. Local information may be removed if app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, depending on Android and the app's implementation.

5. Google services and media permissions

When Google authentication or Google Drive access is used, information is handled through Google's services under the permissions shown during authorization. Access to shared media depends on Google Drive permissions. Users should not share passwords, OAuth tokens, or access to their entire Google account with other people.

Google's own privacy terms and policies apply to Google's services. HomeStream does not claim ownership of users' Google accounts or media.

6. Sharing and disclosure

HomeStream is designed to keep personal playback data on the device rather than sending it to a HomeStream backend. This does not mean Google receives no information: Google processes information as necessary to provide sign-in and Drive services. Do not use the app for information you are not authorized to access.

7. Security

The app is intended to protect stored authentication material using Android's secure storage facilities and to avoid exposing tokens in logs. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

HomeStream is intended for family use. A parent or guardian should supervise children's use and ensure they access appropriate content and accounts.

9. Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on the device until changed or removed by the user or Android. Users can sign out or clear app data through available app and Android settings. Google account data and Drive files are managed through the user's Google account and Google Drive settings.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as HomeStream changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are made.
