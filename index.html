HomeStream is an Android media player designed to help a family browse and play media they are authorized to access, with personal playback information kept on the device.

Read the Privacy PolicyHow it works

Media access

HomeStream can use Google authentication and Google Drive permissions to access media. Access depends on the permissions granted to the signed-in Google account and the files shared with it.

Personal playback

Playback progress, settings, and other supported personal playback data are intended to remain on the user's device. Local data is not automatically synchronized between devices.

Privacy

HomeStream should never require you to share another person's Google password or OAuth token. Review the privacy policy to understand the current data handling.

Availability

HomeStream is currently distributed privately for controlled testing. This website does not provide an APK download or grant access to any media by itself.
